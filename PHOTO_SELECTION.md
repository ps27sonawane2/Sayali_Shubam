## Presentation rules

- All selected files remain in the original JPG format and are copied without re-encoding.
- Every photo keeps its original aspect ratio; no important facial or body details are cropped.
- Gallery cards use varied sizes and spacing, not identical tiles.
- No photo is repeated across sections.
- The family photo caption avoids naming people because their relationships were not identified.
